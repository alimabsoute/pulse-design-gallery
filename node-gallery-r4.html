Strengths

  • Particles make the network feel alive — foodies sense activity before they read a word
  • Full 4-factor breakdown on every connection hover — no data left behind
  • Line width encodes bond strength passively — strongest links are unmissable
  • Dimming on node hover cuts visual clutter precisely where needed
  • Chips at card bottom give foodies vocabulary to describe the connection

Tradeoffs

  • Hover card on mobile requires tap precision on thin lines — challenging UX
  • Many connections visible at once may overwhelm casual users
  • Fixed-position card can clip at viewport edge on small screens
  • Particles add GPU cost on low-end devices with many edges

Intelligence Briefs

Strengths

  • Always-on badges mean zero hover-fishing — every connection is legible at rest
  • Color-coded by dominant bond type creates instant wayfinding across the graph
  • Right panel is a full dossier — perfect for the curious foodie who wants everything
  • Dashed flow animation gives direction without overwhelming the eye
  • Works beautifully as a click-first mobile pattern (node click → panel)

Tradeoffs

  • Badge density can crowd the graph on a phone if connections overlap
  • Right panel eats 40% of frame — smaller graph area to explore
  • Dominant type coloring may oversimplify multi-driver connections
  • Panel close gesture is unfamiliar for users expecting a back button

Strengths

  • Most information-dense design — reveals all four drivers without any click
  • Bundle thickness gives instant sense of connection complexity
  • Fan-open hover is visually satisfying and memorable
  • Square nodes feel architectural and data-confident, unlike generic circles
  • Strand color system is learnable in one pass — applies to the whole graph

Tradeoffs

  • Highest learning curve — requires user to absorb the strand legend first
  • Fan-open animation needs smooth perpendicular math or it looks jittery
  • At small sizes (mobile) strands compress to illegible hairlines
  • Label density on hover can overlap neighboring bundles
