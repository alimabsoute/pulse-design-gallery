The PHL Thesis
Philadelphia is the only market where a single chef network — CookNSolo — can move citywide buzz by double digits on a single announcement. That's not a scene. That's a franchise with editorial leverage.
San Francisco peaked higher on a single event (the omakase boom at +18), but couldn't hold it. Philadelphia's gains are stair-stepping — each event leaves the floor higher than before, which is the only kind of growth that compounds.
New York is the mature market: deep, stable, hard to move. Philadelphia is where the action is. The delta between PHL's Day 0 and Day 90 (+46 pts) is the largest of any tracked city this quarter, and the quarter isn't even an outlier — it's becoming the new baseline.
