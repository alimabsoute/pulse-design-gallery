Why this works
  • Graph hero grabs the eye — cities, neighborhoods, relationships all visible immediately

What I'd steal from other designs
  • From D2: let the flash cards hover inside the node graph at larger viewports
  • From D3: add the 4-metric KPI strip (articles today / avg buzz / top city / fast movers count) between graph and chart
  • From D5: offer a "Focus mode" toggle that pins the graph to the left rail on desktop
