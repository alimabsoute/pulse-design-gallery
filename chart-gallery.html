What this means for Philadelphia today
Philadelphia's buzz has climbed 47 points over 90 days — the strongest sustained run since Q4 2024.

Pros

Multi-city comparison immediately reveals competitive positioning. Toggling isolates a market cleanly without cognitive overhead.

Cons

More complex read, especially on mobile. New visitors may not know what city comparisons mean for their own market.

TC2 Design Direction Two

The Heartbeat

One city. One line. The green pulse reads like a live ECG — sharp peaks where events happened, a trailing glow that feels like it's writing itself right now.

30-Second Brief
Philadelphia food scene is running hot. Buzz has climbed 47 points in 90 days. Three Fishtown openings in May alone. The James Beard semifinalist announcement sparked the sharpest spike on record. The heartbeat doesn't lie.
Pros

Emotionally compelling, fast read. The ECG aesthetic is immediately legible — up is good, sharp peaks mean events. Zero learning curve.

Cons

Single city only — no competitive context. Less analytical depth for power users who want to compare or drill down.

TC3 Design Direction Three

Signal Layers

A stacked area chart that reveals what's driving the buzz — not just that it's moving. Toggle each signal layer to decompose the trend into its causes.

What's driving Philadelphia's buzz index?
↑ 23% vs last month
The story this week
Opening activity is the dominant driver — three Fishtown debuts added an estimated 14 index points.
Award buzz (James Beard) contributed ~12 points at its peak. That signal is now fading.
Closures and reductions subtracted 4 points net. Philadelphia is still up — cleanly.
Pros

Shows WHAT is driving the trend, not just that it's trending. The layer decomposition is a genuinely unique analytical angle.

Cons

Stacked areas are harder to read precisely. Casual users may not understand layering semantics without onboarding.
